# RC4 locked bag artwork

The product-owner supplied photographic bag assets are the RC4 masters:

- `cabin-bag-photo-rc4.jpg` — cabin-bag selector and supporting thumbnail.
- `personal-item-photo-rc4.jpg` — personal-item selector and supporting thumbnail.
- `cabin-bag-measurement-rc4.jpg` — cabin-bag result visual.
- `personal-item-measurement-rc4.jpg` — personal-item result visual.

The former public `cabin-bag.svg` and `personal-bag.svg` line-art files were
removed intentionally. Do not restore or reference them. New consumers must
use the RC4 photographic masters above so selector and result artwork remain
consistent.
