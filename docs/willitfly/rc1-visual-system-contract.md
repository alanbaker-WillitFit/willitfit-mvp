# WillItFly RC1 Visual System Contract

Status: RC1 visual baseline captured from approved reference compositions and the locked globe direction.

## Product shell

- Compact WillItFly header with restrained navigation.
- White/near-white canvas.
- Black/navy-neutral interface language.
- Generous whitespace and clear hierarchy.
- Thin borders and soft, low-opacity shadows.
- Minimal-radius geometry: crisp and engineered, not playful or pill-heavy.
- Simple line icons.
- Colour is functional: flags, governed status, and WillIt green interaction emphasis only.

## Home hero

The approved flat relief-map visual language evolves to the premium white interactive globe. Search remains the dominant task surface. The globe must feel integrated into the same quiet monochrome product family rather than becoming a separate 3D spectacle.

## Destination page hierarchy

1. Breadcrumb / destination context.
2. Destination name and flag.
3. Short factual descriptor and data verification/review line when governed.
4. Five primary summary cards:
   - Power
   - Connectivity
   - Money
   - Entry
   - Weather
5. Secondary detail row for governed topics such as:
   - Language
   - Driving
   - Time Zone
   - Insurance
6. Deeper answer pages and `More about <destination>` questions follow after the summary layer.

## Primary cards

- Equal visual weight across the five-card desktop row.
- Icon first, then concise topic title, then high-value factual summary.
- Details action is visually secondary.
- No invented values: a card may show an unavailable state when Runtime lacks approved data.
- Desktop favours a five-column presentation where width permits.
- Smaller viewports convert the same information hierarchy into horizontally scrollable or stacked/tappable surfaces without changing topic priority.

## Secondary cards

- More compact than primary cards.
- One-line or two-line summary only.
- Chevron/action affordance may lead to detailed answer content.
- Same thin border, near-white background and restrained shadow language.

## Browse destinations

- Search field first.
- Governed regional/category filters may follow.
- Destination cards carry flag, canonical display name and governed geographic parent/region where available.
- Cards remain compact, factual and neutral.

## Responsive behaviour

Desktop, tablet and mobile are separate compositions using the same component language. Desktop should not simply be scaled down. Mobile prioritises search, destination identity and the five high-value summary topics before deeper content.

## Trust and copy

- Short factual copy.
- Avoid promotional superlatives.
- Verification/review wording must correspond to governed data state.
- Missing data remains missing rather than guessed.
- The globe is orientation/feedback; search and destination content remain the actual product journey.
